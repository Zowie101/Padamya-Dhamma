COMMON QUESTIONS

Quick dental care reminders.

Helpful answers to common everyday dental care questions.

How often should I visit the dentist?

+

Regular dental visits are recommended as part of maintaining good oral health, and your dentist can advise a schedule that's suitable for your individual needs.

Why is brushing twice a day important?

+

Brushing consistently helps keep your mouth clean by removing everyday buildup and supporting good oral hygiene as part of your daily routine.

What should I do if I have tooth discomfort?

+

If you experience ongoing discomfort or notice changes in your teeth or gums, it's a good idea to arrange a dental appointment for professional advice.

Are children's dental visits important?

+

Yes. Early dental care helps children become familiar with dental visits while encouraging healthy brushing and oral hygiene habits.
